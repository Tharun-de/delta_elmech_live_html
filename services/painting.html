Painting Works

Interior and exterior painting, epoxy coatings, waterproofing, and scheduled maintenance painting across corporate facilities and industrial sites.

What we deliver

Our civil and painting teams handle the full range of surface finishing works for corporate campuses, IT parks, data centres, and commercial buildings. From routine maintenance repaints to full facility fit-out painting, we use the right materials for each environment.

  • Interior & exterior wall painting — emulsion, enamel, and texture finishes
  • Surface preparation — putty application, priming, sanding and crack filling
  • Epoxy floor coating for server rooms, data centres, and industrial facilities
  • Industrial-grade painting for machinery and structural steel
  • Waterproofing for roofs, terraces, and wet areas
  • Anti-corrosion protective coatings
  • Scheduled maintenance painting and touch-up works across all floors

Process & Quality

  • Pre-work surface assessment and material specification
  • Dust and odour containment protocols for occupied offices
  • After-hours scheduling available to avoid disruption
  • Post-completion inspection with client signoff
Typical Scope

Small floor repaint: 2–4 days. Full facility painting: 2–4 weeks depending on area.

Environments

Office interiors, server rooms (epoxy), parking decks, exterior facades, mechanical rooms.
