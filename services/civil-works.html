Civil Works

Foundations, floors, partitions and finishes executed with quality control and speed.

What we deliver

We build and retrofit civil elements for industrial and commercial spaces: sub‑structures, slabs, partitions, waterproofing and finishing. We coordinate with MEP to eliminate rework and ensure clean handovers.

  • Foundations and slabs
  • Masonry, drywall and partitions
  • Waterproofing and finishing
  • Coordination with MEP services

Unique techniques

  • Laser level checks and slump tests
  • Mock‑ups and sample approvals to lock quality
  • Gantt with buffer tracking for critical paths
  • Dust and noise control plans
Typical timelines

Small floor: 1‑3 weeks. Larger blocks: 4‑10 weeks as per scope.

Safety & QA

Barricading, signage, curing logs, cube strength reports and handover checklist.
