Architecture & Designing

Concept to detail: plans, interiors and coordination for functional, beautiful spaces.

What we deliver

We develop concepts, layouts, working drawings and BOQs; coordinate with MEP and execute interiors with a focus on usability and durability.

  • Concepts, 3D renders and material boards
  • Working drawings and approvals
  • Interior execution and site coordination
  • Handover with as‑built documentation

Unique techniques

  • Design sprints with stakeholder workshops
  • Mock‑ups for critical finishes
  • Material value‑engineering without quality loss
  • Defect snagging with shared tracker
Typical timelines

Design: 2‑4 weeks. Execution: 3‑8 weeks depending on scope.

Safety & QA

Site protection plans, MSDS for materials, and quality stage‑gates.
