Facility Management

End‑to‑end operations, preventive maintenance, on‑call support and energy optimization.

What we deliver

We manage assets, vendors and tickets across electrical, mechanical, housekeeping and security. Our CAFM workflows ensure every job is planned, executed and closed with clear SLAs and reporting.

  • Preventive and breakdown maintenance
  • Asset registry and AMC tracking
  • Helpdesk with SLA dashboards
  • Energy monitoring and optimization

Unique techniques

  • Mobile checklists with QR asset tagging
  • Condition‑based maintenance scheduling
  • Energy benchmarking and retrofits
  • Emergency drills and mock audits
