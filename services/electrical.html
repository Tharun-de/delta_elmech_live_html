Electrical Services

Power distribution, cabling, panels, testing and commissioning — delivered with safety, speed and precision.

What we deliver

We execute end‑to‑end electrical works for facilities and industrial plants: HT/LT distribution, cable tray and cabling, switchboards and panels, earthing, lighting, testing and commissioning. Our teams are licensed and follow strict safety SOPs and LOTO procedures.

  • HT/LT distribution, transformers and switchgear
  • Panel fabrication, installation and energization
  • Power and control cabling with proper routing
  • Testing, commissioning and documentation

How we improve your operations

  • Thermal imaging & load analysis to prevent unplanned downtime
  • Digitized checklists and issue tracking for zero‑gap handover
  • Predictive maintenance schedules based on run‑hours
  • Rapid mobilization with prefabrication and tested assemblies
Typical timelines

Small floor: 1‑2 weeks. Medium plant: 3‑6 weeks. Commissioning varies by scope.

Safety & QA

Toolbox talks, PPE audits, earthing checks, insulation resistance reports, final FAT/SAT.
