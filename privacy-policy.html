Delta Elmech Systems ("Delta Elmech", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It is written for visitors in India and applies to the website at deltaelmechsystems.com.

What data we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to share with us, and a small amount of technical data needed to run the website.

Information you give us

When you submit our enquiry form or contact us by email or phone, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, company or organisation name, and the details of your enquiry or requirement.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log standard technical information such as your browser type, approximate location, and the pages you visit. This is used only to keep the site secure and working.

How we use your data

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide quotes or information you request;
  • To communicate with you about our facility management and electromechanical services;
  • To maintain ordinary business records; and
  • To keep the website secure and improve how it works.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Legal basis and your consent

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you voluntarily submit the enquiry form or contact us, and to pursue the legitimate business purposes described above. You may withdraw your consent at any time (see "Your rights" below).

Third-party services we use

Our enquiry form is provided through Google Forms, and our location map is embedded from Google Maps. When you interact with these, your information is also handled by Google in line with the Google Privacy Policy. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. We do not control how these third parties process data.

Cookies

Our own website does not set marketing or tracking cookies. Embedded third-party content (such as Google Forms and Google Maps) may set their own cookies when loaded. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing your data

We share personal data only with the service providers that help us operate the website (such as Google, as described above), or where we are required to do so by law or to protect our legal rights. We do not share your data with anyone else for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond to you, to maintain our normal business records, or as required by applicable law. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, update, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to withdraw your consent to its use. To make a request, email us at deltaelmech2014@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against loss, misuse, or unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Children

This website is intended for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at deltaelmech2014@gmail.com or +91 9346397285.

This policy is provided for general information and reflects how we currently use data on this website. It is not legal advice.
