Government Liaison

TSSEB, CEIG, Fire Department, SEZ, and Explosive Department approvals and compliance — handled entirely on behalf of our clients so they don't have to.

What we deliver

Statutory approvals and government department liaison is one of the most time-consuming aspects of running a large corporate facility. Our team has 12+ years of experience navigating Telangana's regulatory bodies — we handle all approvals, renewals, and inspections on behalf of our clients, saving their facilities teams considerable time and effort.

  • TSSEB (Telangana State Southern Power Distribution Company) — power connection, load enhancement, tariff matters
  • CEIG (Chief Electrical Inspector to Government) — electrical safety inspections and certificate renewals
  • Fire Department — Fire NOC applications, mock drill coordination, annual renewals
  • SEZ (Special Economic Zone) — compliance documentation and liaison
  • Explosive Department — approvals for DG fuel storage and handling

Why this matters

  • Non-compliance with CEIG or Fire NOC can result in facility shutdown — we prevent this
  • Most facility management vendors do NOT handle government liaison — it's a key Delta differentiator
  • Our Grade A CEIG electrical licence qualifies us to directly interface with the electrical inspectorate
  • SEZ clients have strict compliance requirements — we have active SEZ LUT (No. 413/2017)
Our Credentials

CEIG Grade A Electrical Licence (A/T409 T2-2380), SEZ LUT No. 413/2017, Labour Licence SER/RAN/ACL/RR/28447/2017.

Key Differentiator

Very few FM vendors in Hyderabad can handle government liaison across all five departments. This is a service our long-term clients value greatly.
