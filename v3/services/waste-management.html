Waste Management

Systematic waste segregation, collection, recycling coordination and e-waste compliance for corporate campuses and IT parks.

What we deliver

Proper waste management is a compliance requirement for SEZ-registered facilities, large corporate campuses, and data centres. Our teams handle systematic collection, segregation, and disposal across all floors — fully compliant with Telangana Solid Waste Management regulations and e-waste statutory norms.

  • Systematic collection of general, dry and wet waste from all floors
  • Waste segregation at source — dry, wet, and hazardous streams
  • Coordination with municipal waste disposal and recycling agencies
  • E-waste identification, tagging and safe disposal per statutory norms
  • Waste log maintenance and monthly reporting
  • Environmental compliance documentation for audits

Compliance & Reporting

  • Telangana SWM regulation compliance
  • E-waste disposal records and certificates
  • Monthly waste volume reports for client ESG reporting
  • Recyclable material coordination with certified vendors
Bundled Service

Waste management is typically delivered alongside our housekeeping and facility management contracts for a fully integrated soft services solution.

Compliance

We maintain all statutory waste disposal records — important for SEZ and pharma clients who face regular environmental audits.
